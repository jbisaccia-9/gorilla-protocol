# Vertical Slice: Operazione Porto Nero

## Player Experience

Build one 10-15 minute mission in a rain-soaked coastal intelligence facility.
Agent Bruno enters through a storm drain, crosses a container yard, infiltrates
the communications wing, steals a cipher device, survives an alarm response,
and reaches a boat extraction point.

The mission must support two routes:

- Exterior route: exposed, faster, and combat-heavy
- Interior service route: slower, stealthier, and interaction-heavy

Eliminating every guard is never mandatory. Detection, damage taken, alarms,
headshots, optional relay sabotage, and completion time feed the mission score.

## Encounter Structure

1. Briefing and playable approach
2. Two-guard perception tutorial
3. Container-yard patrol encounter
4. Optional alarm-relay sabotage
5. Communications-wing cipher recovery
6. One authored response wave with a voiced warning
7. Search, combat, or undetected retreat
8. Five-second uncontested extraction hold

Use 8-12 guards in the authored production map, no more than six actively
simulating combat, two fire-token holders, one flanker, and one melee attacker.

## Definition Of Done

- Complete mission playable with mouse/keyboard and controller
- No placeholder character, weapon, or hero-environment mesh visible
- Full first-person and third-person gorilla animation coverage
- All spoken protagonist lines recorded in Italian with subtitles
- Guards telegraph every damaging attack
- Performance and quality scalability modes both meet their frame budgets
- No missing license/provenance rows, redirectors, Blueprint errors, or cook warnings
